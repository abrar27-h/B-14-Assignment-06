# 💪 B14-A6-Fit Log

> **FitLog** is a minimalist, no-nonsense gym companion app built for lifters who want to stay focused, plan their daily routines, and track every single set without friction.

---

## 🌟 About the Project

FitLog was created with a simple goal in mind: make tracking workouts clean, fast, and visual. Designed in a sleek dark mode with electric lime accents, FitLog lets you explore exercises across major muscle groups, build a custom 5-lift workout plan for today, save favorite exercises for future sessions, and get immediate visual feedback on your training volume.

Whether you're hitting chest on Monday or squeezing in a quick core circuit, FitLog keeps your routine organized and hassle-free.

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **🏋️ Dynamic Exercise Library Grid**  
   Browse an organized library of compound and isolation exercises displayed in a clean 3x4 grid on desktop. Each exercise card clearly displays muscle category pills, required equipment, duration, estimated calorie burn, and community ratings.

2. **📊 Live Workout Metrics Dashboard**  
   Track your daily training workload in real time. The "My Plan" page automatically calculates total exercises scheduled, total duration in minutes, and estimated calorie expenditure as you add or remove lifts.

3. **📝 Daily 5-Lift Planning & "Save for Later"**  
   Keep your workout sessions realistic with a smart 5-lift daily cap. Easily queue exercises into "Today's Plan" or save them in a separate "Saved" tab for your next gym session.

4. **⚡ Interactive Status Badges & Toasts**  
   Stay aware of your current plan status at a glance. Real-time pill counters in the top navigation bar show active plan and saved counts, paired with instant toast notifications whenever you modify your plan.

5. **🔍 Detailed Exercise Execution Guides**  
   Click into any exercise to open a two-column detail view featuring key equipment specs, sets and rep ranges, difficulty levels, and step-by-step form instructions.

6. **💾 Persistent Storage & Custom Sorting**  
   Never lose your workout plan on page reload. FitLog automatically saves your plan state to `localStorage` and allows you to sort your scheduled lifts by duration, calories burned, or rating.
